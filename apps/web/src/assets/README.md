# Brand assets

These marks identify catalog providers and model families. They do not imply model
availability, change routing, or indicate endorsement by their owners.

- `openrouter.svg`: the unmodified Grape glyph for light backgrounds from
  [OpenRouter's official brand assets](https://openrouter.ai/brand), downloaded
  2026-10-06 from
  <https://openrouter.ai/brand/logos/transparent/glyph/svg/glyph-grape.svg>.
  OpenRouter asks users to preserve the mark's shape and colors.
- `models/*.svg`: unmodified static SVG marks from
  [Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/tree/c385b2b8d1f9e19aa86e628d4e23c91ee1111a47/packages/static-svg/icons),
  revision `c385b2b8d1f9e19aa86e628d4e23c91ee1111a47`, downloaded 2026-10-06.
  Color variants retain the upstream `*-color.svg` contents; OpenAI, Grok, and
  Kimi use the upstream monochrome marks, which are legible on light backgrounds.
  GLM uses the Zhipu mark. The MIT notice is included in `models/LICENSE`.

Claude's terracotta `#D97757` is also present on its
[official site](https://www.claude.com/). Qwen and DeepSeek are identified by their
own model-family symbols, as seen on [Qwen](https://qwen.ai/) and
[DeepSeek](https://www.deepseek.com/), rather than by the OpenRouter routing mark.
The marks remain the trademarks of their respective owners.
